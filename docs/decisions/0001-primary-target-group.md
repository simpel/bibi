# 0001 – Primary target group: venues that sell conferences with food and drink

- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Status:** Decided

## Context
- The pre-study showed two separate markets: B2B conferences and venue rental, and municipal facility booking.
- B2B is fragmented with low entry barriers and short sales cycles. Hotel chains are already served; venues without a hotel have Lega or generic slot tools.
- The municipal segment is concentrated, bound by public procurement and has long sales cycles.

## Decision
1. The primary target group is companies that sell conferences with coffee breaks, meals and rooms, for example World of Volvo.

## Rejected
- Municipal facility booking and association grants: long sales cycles and procurement lock-in.
- Hotel chains and hotel PMS integration (Mews, Opera): already served by other products, not part of the MVP.

## Consequences
- The domain model is built around quotes, program items, requirements, sheets and configurable items (ADR 0021).
- Municipal features are not prioritised.
