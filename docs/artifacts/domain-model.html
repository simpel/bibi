Market gaps

No Swedish end-to-end solution

No Swedish SaaS connects inquiry, quote, room setups, a configurable domain, requirements per vocabulary value, sheets to a role and deposits with Swish, Fortnox and Visma.

Venues without a hotel are underserved

The hotel chains are taken (Event Temple at Strawberry, Proposales at Elite, MeetingPackage at Scandic). Experience centers, museums, manor houses and restaurants have Lega or generic slot tools.

Requirements that carry through to sheets

Only Venjue (DK) shows allergens per guest carried through to kitchen orders. Lega has special diets on kitchen lists. Okazi takes counts per vocabulary value, without personal data, and blocks the sheet on a conflict.

Configurable domain

Every venue is different: conference estate, museum or boat company. Okazi lets each organization set up its own types, fields, vocabularies, statuses and roles, with versioned sheets and export at handover.

Quick booking with payment

Lega takes inquiries, not bookings. Staff should be able to book small meetings with coffee directly, without an inquiry and quote. The customer pays through a Swish or card link or against invoice.

Open pricing

None of the established vendors publish prices. Venjue and Perfect Venue do and have a free tier, which lowers the sales threshold.
