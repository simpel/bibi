Market gaps

No Swedish end-to-end solution

No Swedish SaaS connects request, quote, room setups, a configurable domain, requirements per vocabulary value, sheets to a responsibility and deposits with Swish, Fortnox and Visma.

Venues without a hotel are underserved

The hotel chains are taken (Event Temple at Strawberry, Proposales at Elite, MeetingPackage at Scandic). Experience centers, museums, manor houses and restaurants have Lega or generic slot tools.

Requirements that carry through to sheets

Only Venjue (DK) shows allergens per guest carried through to kitchen orders. Lega has special diets on kitchen lists. Bibi takes counts per vocabulary value, without personal data, and blocks the sheet on a conflict.

Configurable domain

Every venue is different: conference estate, museum or boat company. Bibi lets each organization set up its own types, fields, vocabularies, statuses, line states and responsibilities, with versioned sheets and export at handover.

Predefined tickets with payment

Lega takes inquiries, not bookings. Staff should be able to create small meetings with coffee in one step from a predefined ticket, without a quote. The customer pays through a Swish or card link or against invoice.

Open pricing

None of the established vendors publish prices. Venjue and Perfect Venue do and have a free tier, which lowers the sales threshold.
