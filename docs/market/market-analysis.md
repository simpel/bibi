# Market analysis – Lega Online and booking systems (Oct 2026)

## Summary
- **Multinet Interactive AB (Lega Online) is profitable:** SEK 64.6M revenue, SEK 19.3M profit after financial items, ≈30% margin (annual accounts 2025-04). Profit has been flat at ≈SEK 19M for three years.
- **Lega is a B2B system** for conference and meeting rooms and venue rental, not a municipal leisure booking system.
- **Two markets:**
  - (A) Conference/venue rental B2B: fragmented, low barriers. **Okazi's choice**, see [decision 0001](../decisions/0001-primary-target-group.md).
  - (B) Municipal venue booking + association grants: concentrated (Pinea, Rbok, Multisoft), public procurement (LOU).
- **Time window:** Adda's framework agreement *Booking and grant solutions 2022* expires 2027-08-27 without renewal.

## Lega Online
- **Features:** venue register with room setups and add-ons, calendars, price calculation with customer price lists and discounts, food & beverage with reports to the kitchen and caretakers, checklists, PDF confirmations, statistics, invoicing.
- **Add-ons:** customer portal (inquiry, not a confirmed booking), digital signing, room signs and entrance screens.
- **Integrations:** Fortnox, Visma/Spiris, Unit4, Hogia, Maconomy, Dynamics NAV, Pyramid, SoftOne, BL, API, WordPress, EduAdmin.
- **Customers:** conference venues, course estates, study associations, public agencies. Logos: Ideon, WTC Malmö, Sensus, ABF, Convendum, Akademiska Hus.
- **Size:** "1000+ users" (own claim). Reasonably a few hundred customer organizations.
- **Price:** not published, sold via demo.
- **Weaknesses:** online booking is an add-on and is blocked after 17:00 in some instances; no visible online payment or lock integration; no public reviews.

- Rough size (own estimate): SEK 45–115M/year in Sweden.

## The Nordics
- **Norway:** Aktiv kommune (municipality-owned, Stavanger/Bergen/Øygarden), BookUp ("100+ municipalities", own claim), Rubic (associations, from NOK 600/month).
- **Denmark:** WinKAS, Conventus, KMD Foreningsportalen.
- **Finland:** Timmi dominates; Varaamo (Helsinki, open source) is perceived as easier to use.
- For the venue segment the Nordics are simpler: no procurement, only language and accounting integrations (Tripletex/Visma in NO, e-conomic in DK, Netvisor/Procountor in FI).

## Recommended strategy
1. **0–12 months:** venues, conference estates, coworking. Direct booking, Swish/card, Fortnox/Visma, published prices, import from Lega.
2. **12–24 months:** deepen partner flow (external restaurant), room signs, locks.
3. **After that:** the Nordics (venue segment) and possibly municipalities after 2027.
- Do not compete with Lega on price alone: a 30% margin gives them room to cut prices.

## Caveats
- Multinet's figures come from registry services (Allabolag/UC, Merinfo, Bolagsfakta); the annual report has not been read.
- Suppliers' own claims (Lega's "Sweden's largest", BookUp, WinKAS, Conventus) are unverified.
- Market size and sales cycles are own estimates.
