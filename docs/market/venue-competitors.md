## Per vendor

**Lega Online** (Multinet, Stockholm). Room rental rather than sales & catering. The portal receives inquiries. Kitchen lists with special diets. Broad accounting integration support. Logos: Ideon, WTC Malmö, Sensus, Convendum, Akademiska Hus.

**VisBook** (Visit Group, Göteborg). Hotel PMS with a conference module (packages with lodging, dinner, activities). ≈800 customers in 2019. References: Hackholmssund, Trosa Stadshotell, Nääs Fabriker. A poor fit for venues without lodging.

**Caspeco** (Uppsala, 280 employees, 6,500 customers). Restaurant system; the booking module handles events with pre-ordered menu choices, Swish/card, deposit, quote → invoice and Fortnox. Conferences are not marketed as a product. Important to watch: already installed at Swedish restaurants.

**MeetingPackage** (Helsinki, $4.6M Series A 2023). Quote and direct booking for hotel meetings. Scandic, Strawberry. No own CRM, no confirmed kitchen logic.

**Proposales** (Sweden). Quote layer for hotels. Stripe payment (1.2% fee), no Swish/Fortnox found. Elite Hotels (40+ hotels), Strawberry.

**Event Temple** (Vancouver). Full S&C for hotels. Strawberry 174 hotels with Mews integration. From $249/month, annual contract.

**Mews Events** (Amsterdam). Acquired Quotelo in 2024. The events module requires Mews PMS. EU/APAC only.

**Amadeus Delphi / Oracle OPERA SEM.** Enterprise S&C for large hotels. Complaints: dated UI, double entry, weak reporting.

**Momentus** (formerly Ungerboeck). Arenas and trade fairs. No Nordic customers found.

**Venjue** (Odense, 1–10 employees). Closest to Okazi's concept: allergens per dish and guest that flow through to the kitchen order, table seating, Stripe prepayment, a confirmed booking becomes an invoice draft. Danish integrations only (e-conomic, Dinero, Billy).

**Tripleseat / Perfect Venue** (USA). Full venue flows for restaurants and event venues. No Nordic presence.

**Agendo / BokaMera.** Generic slot booking with Swish and Fortnox/Visma. The price floor for simple room booking.

**Others to know about:** Bookvisit (Göteborg, hotel booking engine), Venuu (marketplace SE/FI/DK, possible lead channel), Timetomeet (Göteborg, marketplace + booking engine), Event Logic (DK), Anolla, Boka.se.

## World of Volvo
- System not publicly known (contact form, Teamtailor for recruitment, Tickster for tickets).
- The food is supplied by Götaplatsgruppen (Ceno), which "cooks all the food" for the conferences.
- Packages SEK 590–1,016 per person. Up to ≈1,100 people, 7 conference rooms.

## Gaps
1. No Swedish SaaS connects inquiry → quote → setup → menu with allergies per participant → kitchen order → prepayment/invoice with Swish + Fortnox/Visma.
2. Hotels are well served; venues without a hotel have Lega or generic tools.
3. Allergies per participant: only Venjue confirmed.
4. Kitchen order to an external partner: nobody markets it.
5. Direct booking with payment for small meetings is missing at Lega.
6. Published prices and a free tier: only Venjue, Perfect Venue and generic tools.
