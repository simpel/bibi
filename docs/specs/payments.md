# Payments and invoicing through providers

Current behaviour. Why: ADR 0100 (providers as ports), which amends 0028 (Stripe and Connect), 0029 (invoicing), 0087 (Fortnox connection) and 0090 (the venue's own providers). Also 0057 (derived balance), 0058 (after commit), 0077 and 0093 (SIE and VAT). Connections: `docs/specs/integrations.md`. Runbooks: `docs/runbooks/swish.md`, `docs/runbooks/visma.md`.

## Providers
- A provider is one adapter folder in `packages/core/src/providers/<key>/` that declares what it can do (`connect`, `collect`, `invoicing`, `signals`; `providers/types.ts`). Core dispatches through the registry and no code outside a provider folder names a provider (`packages/core/CLAUDE.md`, Providers).
- Built:

  | Provider | Connects by | Takes | Invoices | Pushes |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Stripe | Hosted onboarding (Connect) | `CARD` (hosted checkout) | Stripe Invoicing; not a ledger | Webhook |
  | Swish (Swish Handel) | Credentials (certificate, passphrase, number) | `SWISH` (on demand) | - | Unsigned callback, re-read |
  | Fortnox | OAuth | - | Yes; a ledger provider | Topics stream (worker) |
  | Visma eEkonomi | OAuth | - | Yes; a ledger provider | None; polled by the worker |

- Money stays with the venue: the venue is the merchant on its own Stripe account (direct charges, never destination charges; accounts are created with the Accounts v2 API) and its own Swish number. Bibi never holds order money. Bibi's application fee is stored per payment and shown to the venue.

## Routes
- `PaymentMethodRoute` maps a method (`CARD`, `SWISH`, `INVOICE`) to one provider. No row = the first connected provider that can take the method (registry order); a route to a provider that is not connected is `CONFLICT`, never a silent switch (`resolveRoute`). A provider takes `INVOICE` when it has an invoicing port.
- `paymentSettings.get|update` (`paymentAccount:read|write`) hold the deposit rule and one route per method; `choices` lists the providers that can take each. The deprecated `invoiceProvider` and `paymentOperator` fields read and write the INVOICE and CARD routes.
- Settings → Payments shows the routing; connecting and disconnecting happens under Settings → Integrations.

## Payment requests and attempts
- A `Payment` is the request the customer pays: amount (default the receivable's open balance, never more), one receivable (`quoteId`, `depositId` or `invoiceId`), `publicToken`, status. Methods `CARD`, `SWISH`, `INVOICE` (booked on a provider invoice) and `MANUAL`. Each try at a provider is a `PaymentAttempt` with its own reference, link and expiry.
- `payment.create` inserts the `PENDING` request, then in `external` (ADR 0058) prepares the first routed method's attempt at its provider with an idempotency key; a refused charge leaves the payment `FAILED`. `CONFLICT` when no method is routed to a connected provider, when the invoice is booked in a ledger provider that cannot take the payment, or the order is in a `closes` status that is not `invoiceable`. A change preview never creates a checkout.
- A Swish request lives minutes, so its attempt is started from the pay page (`payment.startAttempt`, SYSTEM only, scoped to the token): idempotent per payment and method while an attempt is still valid or not yet started, and it fails with `CONFLICT` once the payment is not `PENDING` or nothing is left open on its receivable. It locks the payment row first, so concurrent starts never open two attempts for one method.
- The first attempt that succeeds settles the payment and sets its `method`, `provider` and `connectionId`; after the commit its other open attempts are cancelled at their providers. Once a receivable has nothing left open (by any route: an attempt, `payment.recordManual`, a ledger provider's booking), the open attempts of its other `PENDING` payments are cancelled too. A provider that refuses a cancel is logged (`payment.attemptCancelFailed`).
- An attempt that succeeds on a payment already paid stays `SUCCEEDED` and the money is kept: `payment.applySignal` reports `overpaid`, logs `payment.overpaid` and tells the order's people with a `PAYMENT_RECEIVED` notification flagged `overpaid`, so staff refund it or keep it as credit. It is never absorbed silently.
- An attempt that fails, expires or is cancelled ends only that attempt; the payment stays `PENDING` and the customer can start another. Only `payment.cancel` (`PENDING` only) ends the payment: it closes the open attempts at their providers after the commit. Money that still arrives on an attempt of a cancelled or failed payment settles it anyway. A payment never moves backwards.
- A paid payment never moves an order's status.
- Nothing stores a balance (ADR 0057). Paid = `SUCCEEDED` payments minus their `SUCCEEDED` refunds; receivable progress and the order balance are derived (`payment.balance`).

## Refunds
- `payment.refund` (`payment:refund`, manager and admin) refunds a `SUCCEEDED` payment in full or in part, at most what is left. The `PaymentRefund` row is written `PENDING` first, with its provider reference when the provider derives it from the idempotency key (Swish's instruction id, `CollectPort.refundReference`). `external` sends only the refund this call wrote (the newest on the payment; the payment row lock serializes refunds), and the provider's signal makes it `SUCCEEDED`. A definite refusal (`VALIDATION`, `CONFLICT`, `NOT_FOUND`, `FORBIDDEN`, `UNAUTHENTICATED`) marks it `FAILED`; an ambiguous failure (`UNAVAILABLE`, `INTERNAL`: a timeout, an outage, a 409 duplicate, a 429, an unreadable answer) keeps it `PENDING`, logs `payment.refundUnconfirmed` and returns `UNAVAILABLE`, since the money may have left. Stripe answers a 4xx other than 409 and 429 as `CONFLICT`. The payment's `refunds` list shows each one. The payment is `REFUNDED` when succeeded refunds cover its amount. A payment booked on a provider invoice that has no collect port is refunded in that provider.
- A refund made in the provider's own app arrives as a signal and becomes one `SUCCEEDED` row (Stripe's refunded total is turned into the step). The step is measured against the `SUCCEEDED` refunds and the `PENDING` ones the provider already holds (a reference), so a dashboard refund is not lost behind a Bibi refund that never reached the provider or later fails. A `MANUAL` payment is refunded at once, and the money is paid back outside Bibi.
- Each refund has `refundedAt` (the date of its reversal voucher, ADR 0077) and `initiatedBy`.
- Stuck refunds: the worker's `payment.refundRetry` job runs every 15 minutes, per organization as SYSTEM, `payment.retryRefunds` (never registered). Every `PENDING` refund older than 15 minutes is read again at its provider through the optional `CollectPort.refundStatus` (Stripe: by its `re_` id when stored, else among the payment intent's refunds by Bibi's `refundId` in the metadata; Swish: `GET /api/v1/refunds/{instructionId}`, the id derived from the key). `SUCCEEDED` or `FAILED` becomes the `refund.succeeded|failed` signal the webhook would have sent, applied by `payment.applySignal`; still in progress waits for the next run; `MISSING` (the provider never received it) is sent again with the same idempotency key (`payment.refundResent`). A provider without `refundStatus` leaves the refund waiting for its signal.

## Manual payments
- `payment.recordManual` (`payment:write`) records money paid outside providers (bank transfer, cash): a `SUCCEEDED` `MANUAL` payment with `paidAt` and a note, at most the open balance. On an invoice in a ledger provider it is booked there if that provider can register payments; none can yet, so it is `CONFLICT` and staff record it in the accounting system.

## Invoices and credit notes
- `invoice.send` raises the invoice through the INVOICE route (Stripe Invoicing, Fortnox or Visma) after the commit and stores `provider`, `connectionId`, `providerReference` and `providerUrl`; with no INVOICE route the invoice stays in Bibi and the customer pays a requested payment. A failed raise leaves it `SENT` without a reference and `invoice.sync` raises it again.
- `invoice.sync` raises a missing document, then reads `listPayments` and applies each as an `invoice.payment_recorded` or `invoice.payment_removed` signal (a payment appears as a `SUCCEEDED` attempt and payment with method `INVOICE`; one no longer there is `CANCELLED`).
- `invoice.void` voids at the provider after the commit. It is `CONFLICT` once anything is paid or when the invoice is booked in a ledger provider; use `invoice.credit`. Visma cannot void a booked invoice at all. A definite refusal at the provider rolls the invoice back to `SENT` (`voidedAt` cleared), logs `invoice.voidFailed` and returns the error. An ambiguous failure (`UNAVAILABLE`, `INTERNAL`) keeps it `VOID` with `voidPendingAt` set (owned, ADR 0051; the DTO's `voidPending`, shown on the order page), logs `invoice.voidUnconfirmed` and returns `UNAVAILABLE`. The same job runs `invoice.retryVoids` after the refunds: each void pending over 15 minutes is tried again; confirmed clears the flag, refused rolls back to `SENT`, no answer waits. The provider's `invoice.voided` signal also clears it.
- `creditNote.list` (by `invoiceId` or `orderId`, paginated, newest first) and `creditNote.get` (`payment:read`) read credit notes with their frozen lines on every surface (`/api/v1/credit-notes`, GraphQL `creditNotes` and `creditNote`, MCP `creditNote_list` and `creditNote_get`).
- `invoice.credit` (`payment:write`) credits a `SENT` invoice in full or in part (whole `lineIds`, an `amountMinor`, or all that is due). The invoice row is locked first, so concurrent credits of one invoice run one after the other; two invoices credited at once may race for a number, and the unique key refuses the second with `CONFLICT`. The `CreditNote` gets the organization's next number, freezes the credited lines (ADR 0052) and lowers the invoice's `amountDueMinor` and the order balance. At a provider it is `PENDING` until the provider confirms (then `ISSUED`, or `FAILED`, which credits nothing); in Bibi only it is `ISSUED` at once. Money already paid is returned with `payment.refund`.
- A final invoice deducts the paid deposits (ADR 0090): `invoice.create` freezes one `InvoiceDeposit` per deposit that has something paid; `amountDueMinor` is the total incl. VAT minus the deductions and minus credit notes, and is what payments settle and the pay page shows. The order is still billed the gross total.
- Fortnox and Visma rows: each line on its revenue account as the SIE export resolves it, one row per VAT rate for a bundle, each deducted deposit as rows of quantity −1 with a positive price excl. VAT per rate of its `vatRows` on the advances account (ADR 0093, 0106; Peppol refuses negative prices). The customer is found by organisation number, then email, then name, and created if missing as a company with its org no. or as a private person, by the order's effective buyer kind (ADR 0106). Visma uses one article per VAT rate that Bibi creates on first use.
- Prepayment invoices (ADR 0106): a deposit sent (`deposit.send`, `deposit.request`) while the INVOICE route goes to a ledger provider is raised there after the commit: one row per VAT rate of its `vatRows`, excl. VAT, on the advances account, so the provider reports the VAT when it is paid. Card, Swish and manual payments on it are booked there (Fortnox `POST /3/invoicepayments` then bookkeep; Visma `/customerinvoices/{id}/payments`). A deposit whose raise failed is raised before its first payment is booked. `deposit.void` credits it there in full. Not built yet: mirroring payments made directly at the provider onto a deposit, and a retry job for an unconfirmed credit.
- Open for the accountant (ADR 0106): whether the final invoice should deduct the prepayment invoice another way at either provider (built: rows of quantity −1 on the advances account per VAT rate), and whether a private buyer's document may be a receipt (built: a full invoice, marked paid when the payment is booked). Visma codes a row by its article, so there the prepayment and its deduction land on the article's revenue account, not the advances account; the accountant should confirm this or Bibi needs an advances article per VAT rate.

## Ledger providers and the SIE export
- Fortnox and Visma keep the books for what they invoice (`ledger`). An invoice raised there takes only payments that provider receives or can register (`checkLedgerAccepts`), and the SIE export skips their invoices, voids, credit notes and the payments and refunds on them (`ownBooks`, `finance/export-sources.ts`). Stripe and Swish keep no books: their payments are exported. This awaits an accountant's confirmation (ADR 0093 decision 6).
- Credit notes and refunds that Bibi keeps the books for get vouchers dated by `issuedAt` and `refundedAt`.

## Inbound events
- Every provider event becomes a `ProviderSignal` (`attempt.succeeded|failed|expired|cancelled`, `refund.succeeded|failed`, `invoice.payment_recorded|payment_removed|voided|credited`, `connection.status`) applied as SYSTEM by `payment.applySignal`, `invoice.applySignal` or `integration.applySignal`, none registered on any surface. The organization comes from the connection's `externalAccountId` (the one cross-organization lookup, ADR 0004). Dedupe is `ProviderEventReceipt (provider, eventId)`, claimed before applying and released on failure so the provider retries.
- `POST /api/providers/<provider>/webhook` verifies the delivery with the provider's webhook port: 200 for applied, duplicate, ignored and unmatched, 401 bad signature, 400 not an event, 503 not configured. `/api/webhooks/stripe` stays as an alias (registered in Stripe's dashboard). Stripe checks `Stripe-Signature` against the raw body with `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (HMAC-SHA256, 5 minute window).
- Stripe events handled: `checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded|failed`, `checkout.session.expired`, `invoice.paid`, `invoice.voided`, `charge.refunded`, `account.updated` and Accounts v2 `v2.core.account*` (status is read back from Stripe). Others are acknowledged and ignored; `invoice.payment_failed` on purpose, since Stripe retries.
- Swish callbacks are unsigned: the body only names which request or refund to read, and Bibi reads it again over mTLS with the venue's certificate and applies what Swish reports. Unknown Swish numbers and unfinished states give no signals.
- Fortnox pushes invoice and payment events over the Topics stream the worker keeps for every connected company (`apps/worker/CLAUDE.md`); on start, a gap over 14 days re-reads every `SENT` Fortnox invoice. Visma pushes nothing: the `provider.poll` job runs every 15 minutes and syncs every `SENT` Visma invoice, deriving what is paid from the invoice's total and remaining amount, less the `ISSUED` credit notes on it (`listPayments` gets `creditedMinor`), since a Visma credit invoice lowers the remaining amount too, and less what Bibi registered there itself (`registeredMinor`: card, Swish or manual payments with a `SUCCEEDED` registration attempt on that provider).
- Visma `registerPayment`: `POST /v2/customerinvoices/{id}/payments` books a card, Swish or manual payment taken in Bibi on the Visma invoice, so the ledger guard accepts those on a Visma invoice. The registration is kept as a `SUCCEEDED` attempt at Visma under the payment key, and polling subtracts it as above.
- The stream's offset per topic is in `ProviderStreamOffset`.

## Public payment page
- `/pay/<token>` (locale-prefixed, `(public)` group). The token is `Payment.publicToken`: 32 random bytes, base64url, unique, never in a DTO or log. Read through the SYSTEM-only `payment.getPublic` (token to organization); an unknown token is `NOT_FOUND`.
- Shows organization name, order reference, amount, status, each routed method and the lines of the document it settles from its frozen `OrderLine` rows (ADR 0052, 0088; a deposit has none), no customer or order details. A `PENDING` payment past its expiry shows as expired. While `PENDING` after a return from the provider it shows a processing state that refreshes every 3 seconds up to 20 times.
- A Next page is not a `Request` handler, so the context is opened with `runWithLogContext` (surface `web`).

## Deposit rule (ADR 0090)
- `PaymentSettings` holds `depositPercent` (1-100) and `depositMinimumMinor`. `deposit.suggest` applies the rule to the order's live priced lines incl. VAT and returns the basis (`PERCENT`, `FULL` below the minimum, or `NONE`) and `paymentLinksAvailable`. `deposit.request` creates and sends the deposit and a payment against it in one step.
- Dates: `Invoice` and `Deposit` keep `sentAt` and `voidedAt`, the accounting dates of the SIE vouchers (ADR 0077).

## VAT on deposits (ADR 0093)
- `deposit.create` and `deposit.request` snapshot `Deposit.vatRows` from the order's priced lines scaled to the amount; an order without priced lines gives `[]`. A deposit payment debits clearing and credits customer advances excl. VAT plus output VAT per rate; a refund is its negation; the invoice voucher reverses the VAT per deduction.

## Not built
- Fortnox `registerPayment`: a card, Swish or manual payment cannot be booked on a Fortnox invoice, so the ledger guard refuses them there.
- Stripe `refundStatus` and Visma `registerPayment` are unverified against their sandboxes (`// Verify against Visma sandbox`).
- Swish connects by entering credentials in a dialog; there is no onboarding flow with the bank. Swish takes SEK only, at least 1 SEK.
- Visma checks against the sandbox are open (`docs/runbooks/visma.md`); Peppol e-invoicing is deferred.
- Open: the application fee amount, Stripe's EU data handling, the exact Accounts v2 responsibilities for fees and losses, the accountant's confirmation of the ledger and VAT treatment.
