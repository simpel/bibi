# My work (the start page)

Current behaviour. Why: ADR 0110 (amended 2026-11-16). Operations: `myWork.view` and `myWork.tasks` (both `self:read`, view read models, ADR 0071).

## Needs you
My work answers "what must I do", not only "what am I part of". The shell shows **Needs you**, a list of tasks the member must act on, at the top of the notification panel. A task is derived on read from data that already exists (run sheets, holds, line issues, quotes, messages, receivables, statuses), never stored (ADR 0110 rejected a stored task list), and it is gone when the data no longer calls for it.

### Page
- Layout: **Needs you** sits at the top of the notification panel on the right of the shell, with every notification under it (newest first, read on as the member scrolls). The top bar's bell opens and closes the panel; it never opens by itself. When Needs you has more tasks than the member saw when they last opened the panel, the bell's red dot pulses (still, under reduced motion) until they open it. On a narrow screen the panel is a sheet over the page, closed when a row is opened. One search at the top narrows both lists: Needs you by the order's reference, title or customer, or the line or run sheet; notifications by the order's title or reference. My work (`/` and `/my-work/{from}/{to}`) shows the timeline only.
- One row per task, shaped like a notification in the bell (`FeedItem`): the icon of what it is about, a sentence that starts with the verb ("Send Kitchen order", "Fix double booking on Hall A with #1042", "Confirm or release 2 soft holds"), then the order's reference, title and customer and when it is due (`RelativeTime`; "Since" for Waiting on customer). The whole row opens the place to act: the run sheet, the quote, the inbox, else the order. The icon is red in Blocked and amber in Today and Soon; in Late it is a red warning triangle instead of the subject's icon.
- Icons: one per subject, shared with notifications (`components/activity/subject-icons.ts`), so a payment, a run sheet or a message has the same icon in the bell, on the notifications page and in Needs you. An area with a sidebar entry uses its icon.
- Rows sit in groups, in this order, each group shown only when it has rows: **Blocked** (red, stop sign), **Late** (due before now), **Today** (due later today in the organization's zone), **Soon** (due after today, through the end of next week) and **Waiting on customer** (nothing to do yet but follow up). Inside a group, soonest due first, then by reference.
- Dismiss (ADR 0114): hovering a row shows a checkmark. It asks first, then hides the task for this member only, until its facts change (its due time, count, amount, the clashing order or the issue); colleagues still see it. The row stays greyed out until the next page. In the same panel, the checkmark on a notification marks it read at once, with no dialog: the panel lists unread notifications only, like an inbox's unread filter, so a read one stays greyed out until the next page and is then gone.
- Empty: a single line, "Nothing needs you right now", and the notifications below; while searching, "Nothing that needs you matches".
- Sidebar: the My work entry carries a red count of the tasks in Blocked, Late and Today (a red dot when the sidebar is collapsed to icons), nothing at zero. The shell reads Needs you once per page load for the panel and the badge (ADR 0094, not on the stream); while the panel is open it reads it again on navigation, when the panel opens again and when a notification arrives, and the badge follows that read.
- The assistant's `myWork_tasks` tool renders the same list (generative UI component in `@bibi/ui`).

### Tasks
Who gets a task: the member who acts (see "Concerns me"). Run sheet tasks go to the order's main assignee and to the assignee or follower of the run sheet's responsibility; every other task to the order's main assignee. Each kind needs its feature's `read`, or it is left out. Due times use two fixed leads until prep lead times are modelled: **run sheet lead** 24 h and **confirm lead** 48 h before the order's first line start.

| Kind | When | Due | Group | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `HARD_CLASH` | `line.issues` finds another order's hard hold, a buffer or a block overlapping a line still to come, one task per line | the line's start | Blocked | `program:read`, `item:read` |
| `RUN_SHEET_BLOCKED` | the current run sheet is blocked | first line start − run sheet lead | Blocked | `runSheet:read` |
| `LINE_ISSUE` | an active line still to come has an issue from `line.issues` (self-overlap, over capacity, people limit, pool shortfall, outside hours; another soft hold is not one), one task per line and issue | the line's start | by due | `program:read`; availability `item:read` |
| `RUN_SHEET_UNSENT` | the current run sheet is not sent, or changed since it was sent | first line start − run sheet lead | by due | `runSheet:read` |
| `UNCONFIRMED` | the order still has lines in an `expires` (soft) state within the confirm lead of its first line start | first line start − confirm lead | by due | `order:read` |
| `HOLD_EXPIRES` | `holdsUntil` falls within 7 days and lines are still in an `expires` state | `holdsUntil` | by due | `order:read` |
| `UNREAD_MESSAGES` | unread customer messages on the order, one task with the count | now (Today) | by due | `inbox:read` |
| `PAYMENT_OVERDUE` | a sent invoice with money open a day or more past its `dueDate` (as Financials counts it), one task per invoice | `dueDate` | Late | `finance:read`, `payment:read` |
| `NOT_CLOSED` | every active line ended within the last 30 days, and the status does not `close` | the last line's end | by due | `pipeline:read` |
| `QUOTE_WAITING` | a sent quote waits on the customer | when it was sent | Waiting on customer | `quote:read` |

- Horizon: tasks whose due falls through the end of next week, plus every task already due. `HOLD_EXPIRES` keeps its own 7 days, `NOT_CLOSED` its 30. The line checks run on at most 40 orders, soonest first.
- A task never repeats a parent's: an order with `UNCONFIRMED` shows no `HOLD_EXPIRES`; a blocked run sheet shows no `RUN_SHEET_UNSENT`.
- The card flags on the timeline keep reading as now; run sheet, hold, quote and message tasks reuse the cards' flag queries, while clashes come from `line.issues`.

### Operation
- `myWork.tasks` (`self:read`, query, view read model): no input but an optional `kinds` filter; output `{ now, timeZone, tasks: Task[] | null }`, null without `order:read`. A task carries `key`, `kind`, `group`, `dueAt`, the order (`orderId`, `reference`, `title`, `customerName` behind `customer:read`), and where they apply `runSheetId`, `runSheetName`, `lineId`, `lineName`, `issueKind`, `otherReference`, `quoteId`, `invoiceId`, `amountMinor` and `count`. Web, GraphQL (`myWorkTasks`), REST (`GET /my-work/tasks`) and MCP (`myWork_tasks`, its own card) (ADR 0003).
- The start page reads `myWork.tasks` and `myWork.view` in one `Promise.all` (ADR 0097); `/my-work/{from}/{to}` reads only the timeline.

## Page
- A timeline of every order that concerns the member (below), today and ahead, nothing else, in four parts: **Today**, **This week** (the rest of it, weeks start on Monday), **Next week** and **Later**. Today always shows (with a line when empty); another part shows only when it has cards. A heading per day in the organization's zone inside each part, the day's cards under it by start.
- `/:org` reads today to the end of next week in one `myWork.view`. Later reads on as the member scrolls: each time the end of the list comes into view, the next 14 days from `next` (the first later day with a card, within a year) are read and added, until there is none.
- `/my-work/{from}/{to}` is any other window of at most 14 days (inclusive, ADR 0064), in the same parts; it links back to today.
- The assistant's `myWork_view` tool renders the same parts, without reading on.

## Card
- One card per order per day it has timed lines that concern the member. An order concerned as a whole shows all its lines that day; one concerned only through a line (staffed, an item they own) shows just those lines.
- The same card as the pinned and hover calendar cards: the card is the order's day rendered as a booking (`CalendarHoverDetails` detailed inside `OrderDayCard`, `@bibi/ui`), so all three look alike with their own data. Header: order name and reference, customer · why it concerns the member, the status badge always top right, then pin and expand. Stats: Guests, When (the day's span, "Day N of M" above it for an order over several days) and Where (the day's bookable items, `item:read`). No money while collapsed. Pinning puts it in the tray like a calendar booking. Expanding loads the order's facts (customer, contact, assignee, followers, lines, total, balance, issues); the expanded state is kept per card in the viewer's browser, so a reload keeps it, as the tray keeps its pins and shrunk cards.
- The whole card opens the order and hovering it underlines the title; the flag chip stays its own link. The bottom row shows only when there is something: the most urgent flag as a chip linking to what to act on, "+N" for the rest, and "Live" while it is happening.
- Flags, most urgent first: another order's hard hold or a block overlapping a soft-held line (the order's reference); then, only for a member who acts on the order: a run sheet not sent, changed since sent or blocked (`runSheet:read`, assignee of the order or of its responsibility); holds running out within 7 days, a quote waiting on the customer (`quote:read`) and unread customer messages (`inbox:read`), on orders the member is the main assignee of. Order-wide flags sit on the order's first card in the window only.

## Tone
- A search box above the timeline filters it as on the tables (`?q=`): free text over title, customer, number, status and places, and the qualifiers `status:`, `customer:` and `place:`. The server filters the first read; Later filters what it reads on.
- Saved views sit left of the search, as on Orders and Run sheets: buttons that each open a search. A member saves the current search under a name, renames or deletes their own views and removes built-in ones (My work has only All; Orders and Run sheets also Assigned to me and Following); Reset views puts the built-in ones back. A view is lit when its search is the one in effect, compared in canonical words. Views are personal: a user preference `views.<organizationId>.<listId>` holding the ordered list (`SavedViewsSchema`), no row meaning the built-in views.
- Every booking and order card has a plain header ruled off from its body. Its issues are said in words as badges beside the header icons, so a glance down a list shows what needs the member: red with a stop sign when something blocks it (a hard hold clash, a blocked run sheet), amber with a triangle when something needs fixing (any open flag on My work, whether the member acts or follows; open issues on a pinned card). Hovering or focusing a badge explains what it means and what to do. A card with a badge also has a dashed border in the badge's colour (`attentionOf`), red over amber; no badge, no border. Table rows, which have no border, carry a 5px bar on their first cell instead. An order the member chose to follow (Follow on the order's title row) reads Following in its subtitle; a responsibility they follow, often by default, is their work and reads Your responsibility. The detailed card's stats are its status (a badge filled with the status's colour; a calendar card with no order status shows the line's Reservation, the state's own name and colour, instead), guests, when and where, the time drawn as the booking's reservation (below); the booking's value is not a stat, the order's total is among the facts. The same on My work, the pinned calendar card and the calendar hover card.
- Each fact has its own cue, and a card shows every cue that applies (no ranking, so one never hides another): **preliminary** (soft holds only) is an amber clock badge with an amber dashed border; **following** (only an order the member chose to follow; work that reaches them by a responsibility, an item or a run sheet is theirs) has no fill or shadow and a plain inner edge, unless an attention border already draws the edge; **done** (over) is dimmed. Blocks (a hard-hold clash, a blocked run sheet) and needs fixing (an open flag) are the header's red and amber badges; **live** (now inside the span) is a green Live badge by the icons and a green ring with a light running slowly round it (`LiveBorder`, `motion-safe`); an attention border wins over the ring. A card with none of these is the plain card. So a followed preliminary booking is unfilled with the amber badge and border, and a followed clash is unfilled with the red badge and border. On the calendar a booking is soft by its hold and a hard hold overlapping its soft hold on the same item (`markBlocked`) is a red badge; a pinned card shows its order's open issues as an amber badge. A hover card is never taller than the room on screen: it scrolls inside. Colour on an order card is the order status's alone, its badge. Only a preliminary reservation shows on a card: an amber Preliminary badge with a clock beside the icons ("confirm or release it"), with the amber dashed border; a firm reservation, or none, leaves the card plain. Calendar marks keep the calendar's own hatch and hue. A clash hover card (several bookings on one item) has a plain frame: its red header names the clash, and each booking says its own reservation and issues. The words: **Status** is the order's, **Reservation** the line's (sv Reservation, never Läge). A floating legend at the bottom centre (`CardLegend` in the `card-legend` help hint, spec: help-hints) names each cue in one word, with its meaning on hover, drawn with the cards' own surfaces; the pipeline board shows the same legend without Live, and the order table without Live and Following (a row has no fill to leave out). Closing it hides it for good.

## Concerns me
Defined in `docs/specs/assignments-and-responsibilities.md` ("Concerns me"). A member acts on `ASSIGNEE`, `RESPONSIBILITY` and `STAFFED`, and on the run sheets of a responsibility they follow (`RESPONSIBILITY_FOLLOWER`, often by default): that is their work. Someone who only followed the order (`FOLLOWER`) is told, not asked, so their cards carry no action flags.

## Not built
- Untimed lines (no place on a timeline), orders without lines, prep lead times, the responsibility's own name on the card.
- Needs you: per-organization leads, tasks for a line item's assignee, deposits, ticking off run sheet rows, snoozing a task, a list of dismissed tasks to restore, unassigned orders and responsibilities as tasks for a manager.
